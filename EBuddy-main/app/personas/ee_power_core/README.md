# Persona Specification: ee_power_core

## 1. Archetype Profile
- **Target Goal:** Power Systems, Smart Grid & Renewable Energy (`GOAL_EE_CORE_POWER`).
- **Student Profile:** B.Tech Electrical Engineering students preparing for core industrial engineering roles (Power Grid Corporation, NTPC, Schneider Electric, ABB) or GATE/PSU core streams.
- **Key Competencies:** AC/DC circuit analysis, electrical machinery, closed-loop power converters, grid synchronization, and renewable energy modeling.

## 2. Curricular Trade-Offs & Decisions
- **Core Workload Balancing:** Balances heavy laboratory requirements (Machines Lab `EE2P003`, Power Electronics Lab `EE3P006`) with targeted simulation practice.
- **Elective Selection:** Prioritizes `EE4L006` (Renewable Energy Systems) and `EE4L005` (Digital Control Systems) to satisfy elective requirements while directly advancing core goals.
