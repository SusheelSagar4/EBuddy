# Student Academic & Career Planning Survey Instrument
**Target Audience:** Undergraduate engineering students (specifically Metallurgical & Materials Engineering and Electrical Engineering).
**Purpose:** To capture real-world student ambitions, planning friction points, elective discovery challenges, time allocation constraints, and pedagogical preferences to guide the deterministic & adaptive learning algorithms of the Personalised Learning Buddy.
**Ethical & Privacy Note:** Responses are anonymous. No roll numbers, names, or identifiable personal records are collected. Data is aggregated and synthesized for curriculum modeling and persona calibration.

### Section 3: Academic Friction & Planning Pain Points
#### Q5. What is your single biggest challenge when planning your semester courses?

- Timetable/slot clashes between open electives and departmental core courses
- Difficulty finding institute-approved NPTEL / SWAYAM MOOC courses for credit transfer

#### Q11. If an AI Learning Buddy updates your plan because you struggled with a mid-semester topic, what response do you expect?
