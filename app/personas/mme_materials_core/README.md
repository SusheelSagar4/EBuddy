# MME Materials Core Persona (`mme_materials_core`)

## Overview
- **Target Programme:** B.Tech Metallurgical and Materials Engineering (SMMME), IIT Bhubaneswar
- **Primary Goal:** `GOAL_MM_ADVANCED_MATERIALS` (Advanced Materials R&D & Physical Metallurgy)
- **Target Career Roles:** Process/Product Metallurgist, Materials R&D Engineer (Tata Steel, SAIL, JSW, DRDO, DMRL, ISRO).

## Academic Journey & Skill Priorities
1. **Physical Metallurgy & Phase Transformations:** Crystal defects, TTT/CCT diagrams, precipitation hardening, martensitics.
2. **Metallurgical Thermodynamics:** Free energy curves, Ellingham diagrams, binary and ternary phase equilibria.
3. **Mechanical Behavior & Fracture:** Dislocation theory, tensile/creep/fatigue mechanics, Charpy impact toughness.
4. **Materials Processing:** Foundry technology, solidification kinetics, thermomechanical rolling, welding metallurgy.
5. **Characterization:** Optical microscopy, XRD crystallographic analysis, SEM/EDS imaging.

## Constraints & Trade-offs
- Balances high-credit laboratory requirements (Metallography Lab, Heat Treatment Lab, Characterization Lab) with core theory courses.
- Respects the 25 credit institutional ceiling per semester.
