# Persona Specification: aiml_career

## 1. Archetype
- **Goal:** `GOAL_TRACK_AIML`
- **Who:** B.Tech EE or MME student at IIT Bhubaneswar preparing for AI and Machine Learning roles alongside the degree: maths and Python foundations, classical ML, deep learning, projects, specialisation and research exposure.
- **Eligibility:** Open to B.Tech EE/MM students in any year. The full roadmap is long (about 1,600 hours); the planner states honestly how much fits before graduation at the student's weekly-hours cap.

## 2. What the planner does for this persona
- Degree courses are planned first for every remaining semester (prerequisites, credit caps, backlogs).
- Career-track phases from `aiml/output (built from aiml/data/*.xlsx)` are paced into the hours left inside the weekly cap; phases that do not fit before Semester 8 are reported, not hidden.
- A phase or skill counts as *demonstrated* only on assessed evidence (timed quiz, rubric project, mock interview). Self-declared interest is weak evidence and never skips content.

## 3. Files
`persona.yaml`, `requirements.yaml` (mirrors `kb.goal_skill_requirement`), `mastery.yaml`, `milestones.yaml`, `diagnostic.yaml`, `policy.py`.

## 4. Data status
All rows are `draft_for_review`. Placement and admission dates, cut-offs and fees are never stated from memory; unverified facts are shown as "not confirmed yet".
