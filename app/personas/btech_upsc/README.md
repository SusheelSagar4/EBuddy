# Persona Specification: btech_upsc

## 1. Archetype Profile
- **Target Goal:** UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) (`GOAL_UPSC_CSE`).
- **Student Profile:** Undergraduate engineering students in Electrical Engineering (`BTECH_EE`) or Metallurgical & Materials Engineering (`BTECH_MME`) at IIT Bhubaneswar (Semesters 1–8) targeting Indian Administrative Service (IAS), IPS, IFS, and central civil services.
- **Key Competencies:** GS1 (History, Geography, Society), GS2 (Polity, Governance, IR), GS3 (Economy, Science & Tech, Environment, Security), GS4 (Ethics, Case Studies), CSAT Aptitude, Current Affairs Synthesis, Answer Writing Speed, Essay Craft, and Optional Subject Depth.

## 2. Curricular Trade-Offs & Decisions
- **CGPA & Backlog Protection (R2):** Strict safeguard capping weekly UPSC hours (3 hrs/wk in Sem 1-2, 6 hrs/wk in Sem 3+) if CGPA drops below 6.5 or backlogs occur, presenting departmental core fallback (`GOAL_EE_CORE_POWER` or MME core).
- **Workload & Clash Mitigation (R1, R10):** Pauses optional reading and freezes new static topics during placement drives, end-semester exam windows (14 days before exams), and BTP project viva milestones.
- **Optional Alignment (R3):** For EE students, Electrical Engineering provides significant course overlap; for MME students, multidisciplinary humanities/management optionals are evaluated with early lock-in by Sem 5-6.
- **Attempt Horizon (R7, R8):** First realistic attempt is scheduled in Semester 8 or post-graduation based on mock benchmark scores and static syllabus completion metrics.

## 3. Known Gaps and Fallbacks
- Verified cutoffs use official CSE-2025 data; earlier cycle scores remain unconfirmed.
- Degree proof submission timing is subject to CSE-2027 notification verification.
